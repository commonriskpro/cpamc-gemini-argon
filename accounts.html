Gemini 4 Argon (Web)

Connect your Google Accounts with active Gemini 4 Argon access to CPAMC. Enjoy autonomous agent tool calling and thinking reasoning directly inside Codex.

Synthetic Tool Calling

Multi-Account Rotation

Add multiple Google accounts into CPAMC auths for automatic load balancing and quota management.

How to Connect an Account

  1. Log in to gemini.google.com in your browser and confirm Gemini 4 Argon is accessible.
  2. Export your Google session cookies (specifically __Secure-1PSID, __Secure-1PSIDTS, SID, HSID, SSID) using a cookie export tool (e.g., Cookie-Editor) as JSON or header string.
  3. Save the credentials JSON into your CPAMC auth directory: ~/.cli-proxy-api/auths/gemini-argon-<account>.json.
  4. Restart or reload CPAMC. The model gemini-4-argon will immediately become available in Codex.

This plugin operates locally and communicates directly with your host's headless browser instance. No credentials or telemetry are sent to external third-party servers.
